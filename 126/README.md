# Mockery

A city has N squares and every pair of squares is joined by a road of known length.
Stirlitz wants to drive around a closed route through exactly three distinct squares,
and he wants that loop to be as short as possible. Find the length of the shortest such
triangle route.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (3 ≤ N ≤ 100) followed by a symmetric N×N distance matrix with zeros on the diagonal and other entries from 1 to 1000.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the length of the shortest route through three squares.

Examples: the 5×5 matrix from the statement → `24`.
