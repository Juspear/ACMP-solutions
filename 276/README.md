# Splitting into parts

Represent N as a sum of M integers that differ from each other by at most one.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two natural numbers N and M, each at most 30000.
**Output** (`output.txt`): M such integers in non-decreasing order separated by spaces.

Examples: `13 4` → `3 3 3 4`, `72 8` → `9 9 9 9 9 9 9 9`.
