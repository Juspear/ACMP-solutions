# Bolts and nuts

A warehouse had k1 bolts and k2 nuts. Then l1 percent of the bolts and l2 percent of the
nuts were lost. Besides the lost parts, any bolt left without a matching nut (or nut
without a bolt) is useless. Given the price of one bolt and one nut, compute the total
damage: the cost of all lost and all unmatched parts.

**Input** (`input.txt`): first line k1, l1, m1 - the initial number of bolts (100 ≤ k1 ≤ 30000, a multiple of 100), the lost percentage and the price of a bolt; second line k2, l2, m2 - the same for nuts.
**Output** (`output.txt`): one integer - the total damage.

Examples: `1000 10 100` / `1200 20 90` → `37000`, `5000 15 23` / `4000 17 22` → `53600`.
