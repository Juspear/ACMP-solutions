# Arrival time

A train departs at a given time and spends a given amount of time on the way. Find the
time it arrives at the final station (possibly on a different day).

**Input** (`input.txt`): the departure time in the format `HH:MM` on the first line, and the
travel time as two non-negative integers (hours ≤ 120 and minutes ≤ 59) on the second line.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the arrival time in the format `HH:MM`.

Examples: `00:00 / 10 10` → `10:10`, `01:02 / 4 6` → `05:08`, `11:00 / 22 0` → `09:00`.
