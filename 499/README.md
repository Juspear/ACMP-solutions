# Tourist

Gena has three tents: the i-th weighs a_i kilograms and fits b_i people. His class has K
people. Check whether he can pick some of the tents so that everyone fits and the total
weight of the chosen tents does not exceed W kilograms.

**Input** (`input.txt`): K and W (1 ≤ K ≤ 15, 1 ≤ W ≤ 30) on the first line, then a1, b1, a2, b2, a3, b3.
**Output** (`output.txt`): `YES` if such a choice exists, otherwise `NO`.

Examples: `10 10 / 5 5 6 6 4 5` → `YES`, `10 10 / 5 5 6 6 7 7` → `NO`.
