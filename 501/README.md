# Construction

Several private plots and one construction plot are axis-parallel rectangles. Find the
total area of intersection of the construction plot with every private plot, counting
overlapping areas once for each private plot that covers them.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (N ≤ 20), then N lines with two opposite corners X1 Y1 X2 Y2 of the private plots, and a last line with the construction plot in the same format. Coordinates are integers from 0 to 100.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the total intersection area.

Examples: `1 / 15 15 25 25 / 10 10 20 20` → `25`, `2 / 15 15 25 25 / 5 5 12 12 / 10 10 20 20` → `29`.
