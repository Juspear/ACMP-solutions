# Flowers

Three flowers stand on a windowsill from left to right: geranium (G), crocus (C) and violet (V).
Every morning Masha swaps the right flower with the middle one, and every evening Tanya swaps
the left flower with the middle one. Determine the order of the flowers at night after K days.

**Input** (`input.txt`): a positive integer K (K ≤ 1000).
**Output** (`output.txt`): three letters `G`, `C`, `V` without spaces describing the order from left to right.

Examples: `1` → `VGC`, `5` → `CVG`.
