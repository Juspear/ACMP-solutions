# Queue

A bank office has two cashiers sharing one queue: the first opens at 8:00, the second at 8:05,
and serving one client takes exactly 10 minutes. The last client is taken at 20:00. Vasily
arrives at 8:00 and is number K in the queue. Find how long he will wait until he is served,
or report that he will not be served today.

**Input** (`input.txt`): Vasily's position K in the queue.
**Output** (`output.txt`): `NO` if he will not be served, otherwise the waiting time as hours and minutes `X Y`.

Examples: `1` → `0 0`, `20` → `1 35`, `235` → `NO`.
