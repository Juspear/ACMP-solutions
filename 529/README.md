# Segment length

A segment is given by the coordinates of its two endpoints. Compute its length.

**Input** (`input.txt`): four integers X1 Y1 X2 Y2, each at most 1000 in absolute value.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the length of the segment with precision 10^-5.

Examples: `3 4 8 4` → `5`.
