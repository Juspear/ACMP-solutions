# Programmer's day

Programmer's Day is celebrated on day number 255 of the year, counting January 1 as day 0.
Given a year of the Gregorian calendar, find the date of Programmer's Day. A year is a leap year
if it is divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 but not by 100.

**Input** (`input.txt`): one integer, the year (1 to 9999).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

Examples: `2000` → `12/09/2000`, `2009` → `13/09/2009`.
