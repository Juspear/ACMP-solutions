# Riddle

Petya picks two natural numbers X and Y (both at most 1000) and tells Katya only their
sum S and their product P. Recover the two numbers.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two natural numbers S and P.
**Output** (`output.txt`): X and Y in non-decreasing order.

Examples: `4 4` → `2 2`, `5 6` → `2 3`.
