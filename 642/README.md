# Crisis business

A newly unemployed sysadmin wants to open a taxi company. He has S money and the only car
dealership in town sells N cars with known prices. Find the maximum number of cars he can
buy without spending more than S.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N and S (1 ≤ N ≤ 100, 1 ≤ S ≤ 10^9), then N car prices (1 ≤ Ai ≤ 10^9).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the maximum number of cars.

Examples: `5 30 / 15 5 11 10 12` → `3`, `6 18 / 5 10 1 2 1 20` → `4`.
