# Thimbles

A ball is placed under the left of three thimbles. Then a sequence of moves is made:
A swaps the left and middle thimbles, B swaps the right and middle ones, C swaps the left
and right ones. Determine under which thimble the ball ends up.

**Input** (`input.txt`): a string of 1 to 50 characters from {A, B, C}.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the number of the thimble (1, 2 or 3) hiding the ball.

Examples: `CBABCACCC` → `1`.
