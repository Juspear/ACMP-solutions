# Home - School - Home

Every day Vasya rides the metro from home to school in the morning and back in the evening,
using a card for X rides. When the card is empty he buys a new one at the turnstile.
Knowing where he bought the very first card, decide whether there will ever be a morning
when he finds his card empty at the home station.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the word `Home` or `School` (where the first card was bought) and the number X (1 ≤ X ≤ 1000).
**Output** (`output.txt`): `Yes` if such a morning will happen, otherwise `No`.

Examples: `Home 1` → `Yes`, `School 2` → `No`.
