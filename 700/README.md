# Honey

Winnie-the-Pooh collects honey for N days. Every day he fills a pot of V handfuls, but on
the way home he eats some of it: nothing on the first day, K handfuls on the second, 2K on
the third and so on, increasing by K each day. Whatever is left goes into the barrel.
Determine whether he ever arrived with an empty pot and how much honey he stored.

**Input** (`input.txt`): three natural numbers N (N ≤ 300), V (V ≤ 10^7) and K (K ≤ 1000).
**Output** (`output.txt`): `NO` if on some day the pot was empty on arrival, `YES` otherwise, followed by the total
amount of honey stored.

Examples: `15 100 10` → `NO 550`, `10 10 1` → `YES 55`.
