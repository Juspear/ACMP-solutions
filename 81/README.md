# Watermelons

Ivan wants to buy two watermelons at the market: the heaviest one for himself and the
lightest one for his mother-in-law. Given the weights of all watermelons, find the
lightest and the heaviest.

**Input** (`input.txt`): the number N on the first line, then N natural numbers (each up to 30000) - the weights.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the weight of the lightest and the weight of the heaviest watermelon.

Examples: `5` / `5 1 6 5 9` → `1 9`.
