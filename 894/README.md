# Ring

Given the area S of a ring and the radius R1 of its outer circle, find the radius of the
inner circle.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two positive real numbers S and R1 (R1 ≤ 100).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the inner radius R2 with precision of at least 10^-3.

Examples: `50.265482 5.0` → `3.000`.
