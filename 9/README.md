# Homework

Given a sequence of integers, find the sum of its positive elements and the product of the
elements located strictly between the minimum and the maximum. The minimum and maximum are unique
and not adjacent.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N, then N integers; every number including N is at most 100 in absolute value.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the sum of positive elements and the product, separated by a space.

Examples: `5 / -7 5 -1 3 9` → `17 -15`, `8 / 3 14 -9 4 -5 1 -12 4` → `26 180`.
