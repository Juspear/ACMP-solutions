# Three mushroom pickers

Petya, Vasya and Kolya share mushrooms. First Petya gives each of the other two as many
mushrooms as they already have, then Kolya does the same for the other two, and finally
Vasya does the same. After that everyone has an equal amount. Knowing the total N, find
how many mushrooms each of them had at the start.

**Input** (`input.txt`): a natural number N (N ≤ 30000); an answer is guaranteed to exist.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the initial amounts of Petya, Vasya and Kolya.

Examples: `120` → `65 20 35`.
