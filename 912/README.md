# Clothes

Every nation wears clothes of its own color. A city follows the traditions of the nation
that has the most residents there; if several nations share the maximum, the city follows
the nation with white clothes, denoted by 0. Given the colors of all residents, find the
color whose traditions the city follows.

**Input** (`input.txt`): N (1 ≤ N ≤ 10^4), then N colors ci (1 ≤ ci ≤ 100).
**Output** (`output.txt`): the resulting color.

Examples: `6` / `1 2 5 2 1 2` → `2`, `4` / `5 5 4 4` → `0`.
