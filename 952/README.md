# On the bus

A bus ticket costs one ruble. Every adult may take at most one child for free, so an adult
with k > 0 children pays k rubles, and an adult alone pays one ruble. Children can't ride
without adults. Given the number of adults and children, find the minimum and maximum
total fare, or report that such a group is impossible.

**Input** (`input.txt`): two integers n and m (0 ≤ n, m ≤ 10^5) — adults and children.
**Output** (`output.txt`): the minimum and maximum fare separated by a space, or `Impossible`.

Examples: `1 2` → `2 2`, `0 5` → `Impossible`, `2 2` → `2 3`.
